Citation support — independent review

Read the question, answer, and cited passages. Label every case and citation using only this evidence. Judge predictions and suggested labels are hidden. Your brief explanation should address the answer’s claims and reasoning, and any irrelevant citation.

Labeling guide

Supported: every substantive factual claim and reasoning step follows from the cited passages together. Unsupported: some claim or reasoning step lacks evidence or contradicts it. Uncertain: the evidence is ambiguous and you cannot decide. Non-answer: the response does not provide the requested information, including saying it is unavailable.

For each citation, use supports_part if it supports at least one actual answer claim, does_not_support if it is irrelevant or contradicts the answer, or uncertain if undecidable. An unsupported answer can still have a passage supporting part of it. An extra irrelevant citation does not automatically make the answer’s claims unsupported.

Respect negation, units, qualifications, and causality. Do not add an exclusivity requirement that the answer never claims. Shared words, valid identifiers, or an apparently correct conclusion alone do not establish support. Use a short explanation to identify missing or contradicting evidence and ambiguities.

These are development calibration cases, including previously inspected examples. They are not held-out evaluation. Review before opening judge outputs or prior expected labels; note any prior exposure in your explanation.
